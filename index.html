About Protect Environment

We are a global movement dedicated to environmental protection, sustainability, and climate awareness.

Our Mission

Protect Environment is committed to reducing waste, conserving resources, and restoring natural habitats through education, community initiatives, and global cooperation.

Founded in 2012, we’ve worked with volunteers, schools, and organizations in over 100 countries to make environmental action accessible to everyone.

Our programs focus on tree planting, clean energy, recycling, and ocean cleanup.
